# Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard (2020–2021)

## Project Overview
This project analyses Adidas sales performance from 2020 to 2021 using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides insights into sales revenue, profitability, units sold, product categories, retailer performance, sales channels, and monthly sales trends across five regions.
The purpose of this project is to transform sales data into meaningful business insights that can support decision-making, improve sales performance, and identify opportunities for growth.

## Project Objectives
The objectives of this analysis are to:
1. Evaluate overall sales performance and profitability.
2. Identify the strongest and weakest product categories.
3. Compare retailer performance across different regions.
4. Analyse the contribution of Online, Outlet, and In-store sales channels.
5. Examine monthly sales trends while considering regional operating periods.
6. Identify business opportunities and areas requiring improvement.
7. Recommend strategies to improve sales, profitability, and operational efficiency.
8. Establish a monitoring process for future performance.

## Dataset Description
Adidas Sales Dataset (2020–2021), published on Kaggle. The dataset contains Adidas retail sales information covering 2020 and 2021.
Dataset link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/heemalichaudhari/adidas-sales-dataset

## Key Findings and Insights
The analysis produced the following findings based on the dashboard:
Regional performance: The West recorded the highest sales and profit, while the Midwest recorded the lowest sales and profit.
Product categories: Men's Street Footwear and Women's Apparel were among the strongest-performing categories. Women's Street Footwear was identified as a weaker category.
Retailer performance: Foot Locker led in the Midwest, Northeast, and Southeast; Sports Direct led in the South; and West Gear led in the West.
Profitability: The South recorded the highest approximate profit margin of 44.8%.
Sales channels: Online, Outlet, and In-store channels provide different opportunities for reaching customers and generating sales.
Monthly trends: The monthly sales charts show variations in sales across 2020 and 2021. In 2020, the Midwest operated for three months, the Northeast for seven months, the South and Southeast for five months each and the West operated for 11 months. In 2021, all region operated throughout the months.Regional operating periods must be considered when comparing monthly sales, especially for regions with different operating periods in 2020.

## Recommendations
Based on the findings, the following strategies could be considered:
Strengthen high-performing regions: Study the factors driving sales and profitability in the West and assess whether successful practices can be applied elsewhere.
Improve weaker regions: Investigate the causes of lower sales and profit in the Midwest, considering demand, retailer performance, product availability, and operating periods.
Review weaker product categories: Examine pricing, customer preferences, product assortment, and promotional effectiveness for Women's Street Footwear.
Support retailer performance: Evaluate the practices of leading retailers in each region and identify opportunities to improve underperforming partnerships.
Optimise sales channels: Compare channel sales and profitability to guide inventory allocation, marketing, and customer engagement.
Monitor profitability: Track profit margins alongside sales revenue to ensure that growth translates into improved financial performance.
Improve performance monitoring: Use regular dashboard reviews to identify changes in sales, profit, and product demand.

## Conclusion
The dashboard highlights differences in regional performance, product sales, retailer contributions, and sales channels. These insights should be used to guide regional strategies, improve inventory planning, and monitor profitability. Also to make better-informed decisions to support sustainable sales growth.
